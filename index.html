How to use it

  1. Open a midspan. Choose a calibrated main midspan photo in a supported Katapult Pro job.
  2. Switch on Ice Sag. Click the persistent map toolbar. Hover over it for per-wire calculation status.
  3. Review, then switch off. Compare the adjusted labels. Turn the preview off before editing to restore the original display.

See Ice Sag View in action

Know what the preview means

Ice Sag View applies predefined ice-sag adjustments using span-length-based percentage formulas for communication cables and conductor-specific lookup tables for power conductors. Recognized power spans shorter than 100 feet use the 100-foot lookup values as a conservative preview. Unknown inputs are not assigned invented adjustments. This is a visual review aid, not universal clearance analysis, pole loading analysis or engineering certification.

Preview changes are not saved to job records. Read the privacy policy and support guide.
