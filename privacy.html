Privacy policy

Ice Sag View · Version 2.7.6

Purpose

The independent Chrome extension provides a reversible visual preview of calculated midspan ice-sag heights on supported Katapult Pro Maps and Photos pages.

Data processed in your browser

To calculate and display the preview, it reads job identifiers, node and connection references, span coordinates and geometry, trace types and wire specifications, company and wire labels, photo calibration and marker measurements, make-ready moves, and native viewer display metadata. This information is used locally for the preview.

Storage and sharing

Ice Sag View does not send job data to its developer, use analytics, or operate a separate extension server. Local browser storage retains toolbar placement and lock preferences. Clearing the relevant site storage removes those preferences. Preview state is temporary and the extension does not save changes to job records or models.

Permission scope

The extension runs on supported Katapult Pro and Katapult Web Services Maps and Photos page paths. It uses the page context to access the active viewer and render the temporary preview. The extension does not request unrelated browsing access.

Third-party services

Katapult Pro remains subject to its own terms and privacy practices. This website and public support issues are hosted on GitHub. Do not submit confidential job information or personal data in public support issues.

Questions

See the support page to contact the developer about privacy or functionality.
